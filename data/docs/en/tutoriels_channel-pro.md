# What you unlock

- **Up to 20 connected Vinted accounts**, multi-region
- **Autocop**: the bot buys automatically the second a listing matches your rules
- **Multicop**: automate across several accounts in parallel
- **Auto-offers**: automatically send configured offers and negotiate without you
- **Dedicated setup**

## Autocop, your biggest weapon

Autocop is what separates resellers who make a living from those who miss the deals. While others sleep or work, the bot buys underpriced items for them, the instant they're listed.

Warning

Autocop buys automatically with your account, without manual confirmation. Take the time to set your rules carefully (brands, sizes, maximum price) before turning it on: criteria that are too broad can trigger purchases you didn't want. Start tight, then widen.

## Multi-account and scale

With up to 20 connected accounts across regions, Pro lets you scale like a real business: cover several markets, multiply opportunities, and let automation work while you focus on reselling.
