# Frequently asked questions

- What is Fyndit?Fyndit is a monitoring tool for Vinted. It watches listings 24/7 based on your criteria and sends you an instant Discord alert the moment an item matches, so you never miss a good deal.
- What is a Vinted bot?A Vinted bot is a tool that automates monitoring Vinted listings. Instead of refreshing the page by hand, you set criteria (brand, size, price) and the tool alerts you instantly when a listing matches. Fyndit does this live, on Discord.
- Do I need technical skills?None. Fyndit is built to be simple: you join the Discord, set up a monitor in a few guided clicks, and you're off. If you can use Vinted, you can use Fyndit.
- Is the free plan really free?Yes, free forever. The Free plan gives you one monitor and Discord alerts, with no credit card and no time limit. It's the best way to test Fyndit before moving up.
- How do the alerts work?As soon as a listing matches your monitor, Fyndit sends you a Discord notification with all the item's details (price, brand, size, condition, photos) and buttons to act directly. All with zero delay.
- Is autocop allowed on Vinted?Fyndit is first and foremost an alerting and decision-support tool: it notifies you in real time and gives you the information to act fast. Each user remains responsible for how they use the tool, in line with the terms of the platform they buy on.
- Does Fyndit work outside France?Yes. Fyndit works on Vinted across Europe and the USA, multi-region. You can watch listings on several markets at once.
- How is Fyndit different from other bots?Fyndit lives right inside Discord: no separate app to install, your alerts and actions arrive where you already spend your time. Add zero-delay alerts and a real free plan, and you get a tool that's both simple and blazing fast.
- Can I cancel anytime?Yes. Plus and Pro subscriptions have no commitment and can be cancelled anytime, straight from your Whop account.
- How do I get started?Join the Discord, set up your first free monitor, and get your first live alerts. Everything is guided, and our tutorials walk you through step by step.
