# Why Vinted is a goldmine

Every day, thousands of people resell clothes, sneakers, luxury or vintage without knowing their true value. A seller in a hurry listing a pair of sneakers at €20 when they're worth €80: that's a deal to grab. The catch: you're never the only one seeing it.

## The one thing that matters: speed

The best deals are gone in seconds. By the time you refresh the page, someone's already bought it. Serious resellers don't refresh Vinted by hand. They use a tool that watches for them and alerts them the instant a listing drops.

Tip

The golden rule of Vinted reselling: whoever spots the deal first wins. Everything else (negotiating, reselling) comes after.

## Where Fyndit comes in

Fyndit watches Vinted 24/7 based on your criteria (brand, size, price, country…) and pings you on Discord the second an item matches. You can buy, make an offer or favorite in one tap, before anyone else hits refresh.
