# What you unlock

With the Free plan, you get access to **one monitor**. You define a search (a brand, a size, a price range) and the bot watches Vinted for you. As soon as a listing matches, you get a live alert on Discord, with all the item's details.

You also get the livefeed preview: you see deals dropping, in real time.

## The limits of the free plan

The Free plan is built to **explore**, not to scale. Concretely:

- One monitor at a time
- No actions from Discord (buy, offer, favorite are reserved for paid plans)
- No automation

That's intentional: Free lets you test the speed and quality of the alerts, with no commitment.

Tip

Use your free monitor on a brand you know well. You'll see how fast alerts drop, and why serious resellers quickly move up.

## When to step up

If you're getting good alerts but missing deals because you have to open Vinted by hand, that's the sign you need action buttons. That's exactly what Fyndit Plus unlocks. We'll explain it in the next tutorial.
