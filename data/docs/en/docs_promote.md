# Item Promotion

Promote your Vinted listings to the Fyndit community.

The Promote feature lets you share your Vinted listings with other members of the Fyndit community. Use the Dashboard first to reach your sessions, or fall back to /sessions if needed. When you promote an item, it is posted in a dedicated community channel where other users can see it and interact with it.

## How to Promote an Item

1. Run /sessions and open one of your sessions (must be Mixed or Selling type).
2. Click **Wardrobe** to browse the items currently listed in that Vinted account's wardrobe.
3. Select the item you want to promote from the wardrobe list.
4. Review the item details (title, price, brand, size, photos) and click **Promote**.
5. The item is posted in the community promotion channel. This costs promotion points (currently 3 points per promotion).

**Tip** You can also access your sessions from the Dashboard: Open Dashboard

## Promotion Points

Promoting an item costs promotion points. You earn points by liking other users' promoted items. Each like you give earns you points, creating a mutual exchange: promoters get visibility, likers earn points to promote their own items later.

Your current point balance is checked before each promotion. If you do not have enough points, the bot will tell you how many you need and how many you have.

## Community Likes

When you promote an item, other community members can click a **Like** button on the promotion post. When they do:

1. They select which of their Vinted sessions to use for the like.
2. The bot favorites the item on Vinted using their selected session, which increases the item's visibility and engagement metrics on Vinted.
3. The liker earns promotion points.
4. The like count on the promotion post updates in real-time.

## Anti-Abuse Protections

- **No self-liking** - You cannot like your own promoted items. The bot prevents this automatically and shows an error if you try.
- **No double-liking** - Each user can only like a promoted item once. Subsequent clicks are ignored with a notification that you already liked this item.
- **Session required** - You must have at least one initialized (authenticated) session to like promoted items.
- **Point cost** - The promotion cost prevents spam. You need to actively participate by liking other items to earn enough points.

## What To Expect

- Promotion happens from a Mixed or Selling session only.
- Other users can like your item to help it get more attention.
- If you do not have enough points, the bot tells you before the item is posted.

## Troubleshooting

- **Wardrobe button is disabled** - Your session type is set to "Buying". Change it to "Mixed" or "Selling" to enable wardrobe access.
- **"Insufficient points"** - You do not have enough promotion points. Like other users' promoted items in the community channel to earn more.
- **Wardrobe is empty** - The session's Vinted account has no active listings. List items on Vinted first, then they will appear in the wardrobe.
