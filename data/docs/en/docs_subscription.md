# Subscription & Billing

How to subscribe, manage your plan, and claim Discord access after checkout.

Fyndit uses Whop for subscription billing. If you want to check your current usage first, use the Dashboard or the /my_info fallback command, then compare it with the tier table in Roles, Permissions & Limits.

## Available Plans

Fyndit offers multiple subscription tiers:

- **Free** - Lets you try the interface and keep a very small setup. Good for exploring, not for a full buying workflow.
- **Plus** - The full personal plan. You get 1 Vinted session, enough monitor space for one serious setup, and the automation features most users want.
- **Pro** - The heavy-use plan. You get many sessions, the highest limits, webhooks, priority autocop, and priority support.

## The Simple Difference

| Tier | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Free | You can look around and understand the workflow, but you do not get a real setup for buying on Vinted. |
| Plus | You get one complete personal setup: one Vinted account, real monitors, auto actions, and everything needed for normal use. |
| Pro | You get the same features as Plus, but with much higher limits for power users, plus webhooks and priority support. |

**Tip** Plan limits apply to active (non-archived) items only. If you downgrade, excess items are archived automatically.

## Where To Buy

Use the pricing section on the Fyndit site to choose Plus or Pro:

- Open Fyndit pricing

What happens:

1. Choose Plus or Pro.
2. Choose monthly or 3-month billing.
3. Enter your email on Fyndit.
4. Complete the embedded Whop checkout without leaving the site.
5. Use the Discord claim button shown after payment.

## Claim Discord Access

After payment, Fyndit shows a one-time Discord claim button. Click it and authorize the Discord account you use for Fyndit. The bot then adds you to Discord and assigns the matching paid role.

If you close the page too early, return to the checkout completion page from the same browser tab or contact support with your checkout email.

Free access still starts by joining Discord directly.

## Cancel Your Sub Anytime

You can cancel whenever you want from your Whop orders page:

- Open Whop orders

What happens after cancellation:

1. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
2. At the end of that period, your access drops back to Free.
3. Any extra sessions, groups, or monitors are archived automatically if they no longer fit your plan.
4. You can resubscribe later and bring your setup back.

## Payment Issues

If a payment fails (e.g., expired card), Whop will retry automatically over a few days. During this time:

- Your subscription stays active during the retry period.
- The bot sends you a DM about the failed payment.
- If all retries fail, the subscription is canceled and your role is downgraded.

Update your payment method via **Manage on Whop** in your Whop account.

## What To Expect

- If you upgrade, your limits increase right away.
- If you downgrade, extra items are paused rather than deleted.
- If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period.
