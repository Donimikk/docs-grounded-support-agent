# Step 1: Create a Discord account

If you don't have a Discord account yet, head to discord.com and click "Register". You can create your account in seconds with your Google address or a regular email.

Info

Discord is a free messaging and community app. It works on phone and computer. Use whatever suits you.

## Step 2: Join the Fyndit server

Once your account is created, click the Fyndit invite link. The server opens automatically and you join the community in one click. You land straight in the welcome area.

## Step 3: Explore the channels

A Discord server is organized into "channels", each with a specific role: announcements, alerts, support, tutorials. Take a minute to look around. Everything is arranged so you find your way fast.

## Step 4: Set up your first free monitor

The free plan gives you access to one monitor. Follow the bot's instructions in the dedicated channel to define your first search (for example: a specific brand and size). As soon as an item matches, the alert drops on Discord.

Tip

Start simple: one monitor on a brand you know well. You'll fine-tune your settings once you've seen how alerts come in.

## You're all set

Your account is created, you're in the server, your first monitor is running. You now get deals live. To go further (autocop, multi-account, auto-offers), the next tutorials and the Plus and Pro plans are waiting.
