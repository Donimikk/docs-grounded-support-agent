# Hidden Item Watcher

Keep track of hidden listings until they become available again.

Some Vinted listings are marked as hidden. When that happens, the normal buying buttons may disappear and the bot may show a **Watch Hidden** button instead.

## What Watch Hidden Does

- It saves the hidden listing to your account so the bot can keep checking it for you.
- It does **not** buy the item immediately.
- It does **not** make the item visible sooner.
- It is useful when you want to keep an eye on a listing until Vinted allows it again.

## Where You See It

- On a hidden item notification.
- Usually beside the **View** button, and sometimes with a **Block** button.
- If the item is too restricted for your plan, you may see an upgrade prompt instead.

## What Success Looks Like

- You get a confirmation message that the watch was added.
- The item remains on your radar until it becomes available again.

## What Failure Usually Means

- Your role does not include hidden-item access.
- You reached your hidden watch limit.
- The item is no longer available to track.

## What To Do Next

- If the item becomes visible again, use the normal item buttons from the notification.
- If you keep hitting a limit, remove an older hidden watch first or ask support.
- If you cannot see the button at all, check your tier in **My Info** or the tier table in this manual.
