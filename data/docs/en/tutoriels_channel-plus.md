# What you unlock

- **50 monitors**: watch fifty searches in parallel (different brands, sizes, categories)
- **1 connected Vinted account**: link your account to the bot once and for all
- **Unlimited alerts** and instant livefeed
- **Manual actions from Discord**: buy, make an offer or favorite in one tap, without ever opening Vinted
- **Chat support**

## Why it's a game-changer

On the Free plan, you see the deal but you have to rush to Vinted to buy it. And often, it's too late. With Plus, the **Buy** button is right in the Discord alert. You click, it's yours. That's where Fyndit's speed turns into real money.

Tip

The 50 monitors let you cover several niches at once. Many resellers dedicate a few monitors to their safe brands, and the rest to hunting underpriced listings.

## Going further

Plus gives you manual actions. But if you want the bot to buy **on its own**, without you clicking, you need the full automation of the Pro plan. That's the topic of the last tutorial.
