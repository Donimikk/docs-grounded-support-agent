# 1. What exactly is Vinted reselling?

The principle is simple: buy an item below its real value, then resell it at its fair price. The difference is your margin. Vinted is a goldmine because thousands of sellers list every day without knowing the real value of their items: sneakers, streetwear, luxury, vintage.

## 2. Pick your niche

Don't go in every direction. Resellers who win specialize: one or two categories they know inside out (men's streetwear, or designer bags, for example). Knowing your niche lets you spot a deal in a second, where a generalist hesitates.

Tip

Start with what you already know. If you know sneakers, you'll spot an underpriced pair far faster than any algorithm.

## 3. Sourcing: finding the deals

Sourcing is the heart of the job. Two realities: the best deals are gone in seconds, and you're never the only one seeing them. Refreshing Vinted by hand means losing every time. Serious resellers use a monitoring tool that watches listings for them and alerts them the instant an item matches their criteria.

## 4. Speed, your real edge

On Vinted, whoever spots the deal first wins. A few seconds are the difference between a double-digit margin and a missed chance. That's exactly the problem Fyndit solves: zero-delay alerts, pushed to Discord, with the ability to buy in one tap before anyone else.

## 5. Negotiate and buy smart

On Vinted, you can make offers. A polite, reasonable offer is often accepted, especially on items listed for a while. For real deals though, don't negotiate: buy immediately, someone else might already be doing it.

## 6. Resell with margin

Once you've bought the item, take care with your resale: clean photos, honest description, price aligned with the market. Consistency pays more than isolated big hits. Many resellers reinvest their first margins to scale up volume.

## 7. Scaling up

Once you master the basics, you can industrialize: several niches, several accounts, automated alerts and buys. That's when reselling goes from side-business to real activity. Tools like Fyndit Pro (multi-account, autocop, multi-region) are built for this stage.
