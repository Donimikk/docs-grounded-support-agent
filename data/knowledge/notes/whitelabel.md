# Whitelabel

The operator of another Discord can have the Fyndit bot deployed on their server
under their own brand. There is **not a single line** about it in the
documentation, and yet people ask regularly - two of eight tickets in one batch.

## Pricing

- **€150 a month** - the basic whitelabel. Includes autocop and private monitors
  for the members of their server.
- **€100 one-off** for the initial installation of the bot on their server.
- **€80 a month** - the cheaper version, when public channels in a new bot are
  enough for them (pro tier). Also suitable for their own buying.

The bot currently runs this way for **13 other communities**.

## When to mention it at all

**Only when somebody asks about their own community or their own server.** Do not
offer it unprompted. An ordinary customer asking about monitors, pricing or
setup does not want whitelabel, and mentioning it only confuses them - it looks
as if we were selling them something more expensive than they needed.

The giveaway is wording like *"our server", "our community", "for my members"* or
a direct question about having their own bot.

## How to handle such a ticket

**It is a commercial conversation and it belongs to the owner.** Repeat the
prices above by all means, they are published rates and the person is asking
about them. But everything else - whether the installation fee can be waived,
individual terms, technical details of the deployment, comparisons with their
current provider - is the owner's decision.

Do not promise a discount or a waived fee. Even if the server was with us in the
past, renewing it is the owner's call.

## Unrelated things with a similar name

Whitelabel is **deploying the bot on someone else's server**. It has nothing to
do with the whitelist in a monitor's filters. If somebody asks about a
"whitelist", they mean keywords, not this.
