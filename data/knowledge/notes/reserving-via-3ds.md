# An item can be reserved, but not with a button

Fyndit has **no "reserve" feature**. Even so, the question "can an item be held
while I decide?" is a fair one - it can, by deliberately leaving the 3DS step
hanging.

While 3DS verification is pending, **Vinted holds the item for 15 minutes** and
nobody else buys it in the meantime. That is the only timer that makes the
reservation, and it belongs to Vinted, not to us.

## The procedure

1. **Freeze the card** in the banking app (the guide is written for a bank where
   that is one button).
2. Start the purchase through the bot as usual.
3. The payment does not pass 3DS and the item switches to **"Item Reserved"**.
   The 15 minutes start now.
4. If the person decides to buy: hit **"Complete payment"** and confirm in the
   banking app through **"Ignore freeze & confirm"**.
5. If not: do nothing and the reservation expires by itself after 15 minutes.

Several items can be reserved at once. According to the guide there is no penalty
for an unfinished purchase - unlike buying and then cancelling, which raises the
risk of a shadow ban.

## When they ask "how long does it hold", the answer is 15 minutes

**Say the number.** While 3DS verification is pending, Vinted holds the item for
**15 minutes** - that is exactly what the person wants to know, and it is
confirmed.

Measured: asked how much time they had to check a listing, the model described
only Fyndit's own short countdown in 2 of 3 answers and **never mentioned the 15
minutes** - even while citing this note. Out of two numbers it picked the safer
one. The caveat below used to be written before the answer and crowded it out.

### Only then the caveat: there are two timers

- **The 15 minutes are Vinted's** - the time it holds an item for 3DS.
- **Fyndit has its own, shorter countdown** (`docs_3ds.md`: "a few minutes"),
  after which the turn passes to the next in the queue.

They are two numbers about two different things and both belong in the answer -
in this order. The answer must not claim that Fyndit has a reserve button, nor
that it sets any limit itself. But staying silent about the 15 minutes for fear
of mixing them up leaves the customer without an answer we have.

## Where each part comes from

Confirmed by the team are **those 15 minutes on Vinted's side**. The rest of the
procedure - freezing the card, "Item Reserved", the claim about a lower shadow
ban risk - is a community guide circulating among users. It is not official
Fyndit documentation and it should be described as such: a method people use, not
a feature we promise.
