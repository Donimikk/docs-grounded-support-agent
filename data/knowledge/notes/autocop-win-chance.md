# There is no success rate

**No success-rate figure exists.** Not in the documentation, not with the owner,
not anywhere. Nobody has one, so there is nothing to go and find out.

This matters because the question almost always arrives shaped like a number —
*"what's the success rate if 80 people go for the same item"* — and the tempting
reply is to promise to look the number up. **Never promise that.** It sends the
customer away expecting a figure, and leaves the owner having to either invent
one or look like he does not know his own product.

Answer with the mechanism instead. It is a real answer and the customer can act
on it.

## What is actually true

**Identical setups have identical odds.** Two people on this same Discord, both
on Pro, both with autocop, going for the same item are on equal footing. The
chance is simply shared between everyone attempting it.

The documentation describes how that sharing works: matching autocop monitors
form a queue, position 1 gets the attempt, and if it fails, is declined, or 3DS
times out, the next person is promoted automatically.

So a plan does not win a race against an identical setup. Autocop beating a
manual click is the obvious part; **who else is racing** is the part that
decides it.

## What to say

That nobody has an advantage when the setup is the same, and the chance is split
between everyone competing for it. Then, if it helps them, what they *can*
change on their side — payment method, delivery method, more connected accounts.
Those live in [[delivery-speed]].

## Do not

- Do not give a percentage, a range, or a "usually around" figure.
- Do not promise to find one out.
- Do not imply that upgrading improves odds against an identical setup. It does
  not, and a customer who buys on that promise comes back angry.
