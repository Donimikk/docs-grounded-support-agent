# One subscription, one account

**One subscription is always one Discord account.** It cannot run on two accounts
at the same time, whoever owns them — a second account of the customer's own is
still a second account.

**It can be moved.** Transferring a subscription to another Discord account is
possible, but the Plus role **ends on the first account** when it does. There is
no way to have it on both.

Why this note exists: the documentation never states the rule outright. It says
the claim button binds the paid role to the account authorized at checkout, that
accounts are personal, and that transferring access without written permission is
prohibited — three separate places that only *imply* the answer. That is not
enough to answer a direct question from, so the rule is recorded here.

## How to handle the ticket

Say plainly that it runs on one account, and that moving it means losing it on
the old one. Then point them at support to actually do the move — the transfer
itself is not something a helper can perform.

Do not present this as something the customer did wrong. Asking whether a plan
covers a second account of your own is a reasonable question, not an attempt to
share a subscription.
