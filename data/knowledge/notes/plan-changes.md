# Changing plan mid-period

It comes from people who have just bought Plus and want Pro a day or two later.
The question is always the same: *"do I pay the full price, or do those days
count towards it?"*

## What holds

**Pro can be bought straight away**, there is no need to wait for the Plus period
to end.

**A cancelled subscription runs to the end of the period paid for.** Cancelling
does not remove access immediately - it only stops the next payment. So **the
order does not matter**: they can buy Pro first and cancel Plus after, or the
other way round.

**The remaining Plus days can be recalculated into Pro days - but that is not a
rule.** It is stated nowhere and published nowhere; it is **the owner's
customary practice**, not a customer's entitlement. Until it settles, do not
present it as what will happen. Say you will ask, and let the owner decide.

It is done by hand by the owner, not by the system. In that ticket the owner did
it and wrote *"I recalculated your Plus tier into Pro tier days"*.

## How to talk about it

Say the first two things plainly - they are facts about how the subscription
works.

Be careful with the recalculation. **It is not an automatic feature**, it is the
owner stepping in. Say it is possible and **pass it to the owner** - do not
promise a number of days or a date. The same rule as with refunds: a helper does
not decide what gets credited to whom.

The discount code also works when moving up a plan, not only on a first purchase.

## What you will and will not find in the documentation

`docs_premium-limits.md` describes what happens on a **downgrade** - what gets
archived when a limit is exceeded. About moving up mid-period, or about
recalculating days, there is **nothing** there.
