# Watch and autocop get confused

The model mixes them up and we have the evidence: in a ticket where the customer
asked why **autocop** had not bought an item, they got an explanation about
**Watch Hidden** - three times in a row, even after writing that it still did not
work.

## The difference

**Watch Hidden** is about hidden items. It saves a listing Vinted is not showing
right now and watches for it to reappear. It does not buy.

**Autocop** is automatic buying of items that pass a monitor's filters. It is
**Pro** only.

They are separate features on separate documentation pages. The fact that both
relate to whether a button appears on an item does not make them the same thing.

## Why to watch out for it

When somebody asks why **autocop did not fire**, an explanation about Watch
Hidden is not an answer - even when it is true in itself. Afterwards the customer
knows about a feature they did not ask about, and their problem is untouched.

For such a question the Watch Hidden page looks related, because it talks about
actions not appearing on an item. That is the most tempting trap: **an adjacent
topic reads as an answer**.

## What to do about it

When a customer reports that autocop did not fire on a specific item, the
documentation does not explain why. **Admit the gap and escalate** - the owner
treated it as a bug and shipped an update.

If you are not sure which of the two the customer means, ask. That is not
dodging; they really are two different features and answering about the wrong one
does not help them.
